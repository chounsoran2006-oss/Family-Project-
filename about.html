Our Family in Oddar Meanchey

My family lives in Oddar Meanchey Province, Cambodia, in a peaceful and caring home built on respect, love, and support. We are five people: Chuon, Em, Phorin, Sarith, and Soran.

Our family means more than just living together. It means sharing each step, comforting one another in difficult times, and celebrating the moments that make life beautiful. Every day, we grow stronger by being honest, patient, and present for each other.
